Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 October 2026

Pacific Islands Internet Exchange Incorporated ("PACIXP", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard personal and technical information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information in the following ways:

  • Membership Applications: organisation name, AS number, PeeringDB link, the name and email address of a contact person, location, peering policy, and any notes you add.
  • Enquiries: your name, email address, organisation (optional), subject and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Technical Data: information related to network peering, traffic statistics (aggregated), and connection metadata required for the operation of the IXP.
  • Website Visits: technical and usage information described in section 2.

2. Website, Cookies and Analytics

Hosting. Our website and form service run on Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and to protect the site from attacks.

Form submissions. When you submit a form, its contents are sent to our email inbox. Our form service also logs the IP address, approximate location and browser type of each submission, which we use to detect and block abuse. These logs do not contain what you wrote in the form, and they are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics, delivered through Cloudflare Zaraz, to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, how visitors arrived, and their device type, browser and approximate location. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. We use this information in aggregate only and do not use it to identify you or for advertising.

The analytics service sets the following cookies on pacixp.net:

Google Analytics may also store a small item in your browser's local storage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website and its forms work without them.

Fonts and images. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page loads a photo from Unsplash. To fetch them, your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address and browser details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and assess membership applications.
  • Facilitate internet exchange services and peering between members.
  • Communicate regarding technical maintenance, governance, and regional initiatives.
  • Improve the performance and resilience of the regional network, and of this website.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations as an incorporated society in New Zealand.

4. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Other members (only as necessary for peering coordination, such as PeeringDB updates).
  • Service providers who hold or process information on our behalf (listed below).
  • Legal authorities if required by New Zealand law.

Our service providers are:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, security, form processing and analytics delivery.
  • Resend — delivers form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google — our email (Google Workspace), Google Analytics and Google Fonts.
  • Unsplash — hosts the photo on our home page.

These providers are based in the United States and may store or process information outside New Zealand. They act on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments.

5. How Long We Keep Information

  • Enquiries and applications: kept in our email only as long as we need them to respond, to assess and administer membership, and to keep the records an incorporated society must keep.
  • Form service logs: deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Analytics: Google keeps analytics data for the period set in our account, which is at most 14 months. Cookies last for the periods listed in section 2.

6. Data Sovereignty & Security

As a promoter of digital sovereignty, we take security seriously. We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website and forms are served only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access any personal information we hold about you and request corrections. To exercise these rights, or to contact our privacy officer, please email info@pacixp.net. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be communicated to all active members via their registered contact email.
